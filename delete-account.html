Soul Shelf — Account and Data Deletion

Users of Soul Shelf can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Email aishwaryasri234@gmail.com with the subject Soul Shelf Account Deletion Request.

Include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password.

What data is deleted?

After verifying the request, we will delete the account and associated personal data that we control, including account-related information and personal pantry or shopping list data, where applicable.

Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Processing time

We will review your request and communicate the expected deletion timeline by email.
