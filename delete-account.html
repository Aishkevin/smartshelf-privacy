Soul Shelf — Account and Data Deletion Users of Soul Shelf can request deletion of their account and associated personal data. How to delete your account Option 1: From the app If available in your version of Soul Shelf, open Profile or Settings and select Delete Account, then follow the instructions. Option 2: Request deletion by email Email aishwaryasri234@gmail.com with the subject Soul Shelf Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your Soul Shelf account so we can identify it. Do not send your password. What data will be deleted? After verifying the request, we will delete the account and associated personal data that we control, including account-related information and personal pantry or shopping-list data, where applicable. Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained data will be handled according to our privacy policy. Processing time We will review the request and communicate the expected deletion timeline by email.
