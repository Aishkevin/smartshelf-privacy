1. Information We Collect

A. Account information

  • Email address and password: If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email and password credentials to authenticate you and support account access.
  • Anonymous ID: The App may create an anonymous Firebase user ID so you can use supported features before signing in.
  • Profile details: Your display name, shelf role (such as Owner or Member), and optional avatar emoji.

B. Content you create

  • Pantry items, quantities, units, categories, expiry dates, shopping lists, activity logs, shelf codes and join requests.

C. Photos and camera

  • Receipt or bill scanning: When you scan a grocery bill, the image is sent to our Firebase Cloud Function, which sends it to Google's Gemini API to extract item information. We may store the extracted item list to avoid processing the same receipt again and maintain a daily scan count to prevent abuse.
  • Barcode scanning: The camera reads product barcodes so the App can look up and fill in item details.

D. Voice input

  • When you tap the microphone, the App uses your device's Google speech recognition service to convert supported speech, including English or Tamil, into text. The App does not itself record or store your audio. The speech service may process data according to its own settings and privacy policy.

E. AI assistant requests

  • When you use AI features, such as recipe suggestions or the assistant, your question and relevant inventory details (such as item names and quantities) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. We do not intentionally send your email address or name as part of these AI requests.

F. Advertising and device information

  • The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses and other device or usage information to deliver ads and measure performance. You may be able to reset your advertising ID or change personalized-ad settings through Android settings.

G. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location through the App.
  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS messages or call history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App and synchronize pantry items, shopping lists and shared shelves.
  • To provide AI features, receipt scanning, barcode lookup and recipe suggestions.
  • To send local notifications about low stock and items nearing expiry.
  • To secure accounts, prevent abuse and apply usage limits.
  • To troubleshoot errors and improve the App.
  • To display advertisements through AdMob.

3. Third-Party Services

Soul Shelf uses third-party services to provide its features. These services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Remote Config. Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google Gemini API: Receipt extraction and AI-generated responses. Gemini API Terms
  • Google speech recognition: Speech-to-text input. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising and ad measurement. Google Advertising Policies

We do not sell your personal information or share it with other companies for their own independent marketing purposes. Third-party providers may process information to deliver their services.

4. Sharing Within Your Shelf

Shelves are collaborative. People who join your shelf may see its items, shopping list, activity log and the display names of members on that shelf.

Joining a shelf requires its code and approval from the shelf owner. Only share your shelf code with people you trust.

5. Device Permissions

  • Camera: For scanning receipts and product barcodes.
  • Notifications: For low-stock and expiry reminders.
  • Internet: For synchronization and online AI features.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. The App may continue to work without camera permission, but camera-based scanning will not be available.

6. Data Storage, Security and Retention

  • App data is stored using Google Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Data may be processed on servers outside your country, subject to applicable provider practices and requirements.
  • Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Firestore security rules are intended to restrict access to shelf data to authorized members.
  • We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the App and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.
  • No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data.

In the App: Open Profile or Settings and tap Delete Account, if this feature is available in your installed version.

By email: If you cannot access the App, email aishwaryasri234@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, preferably from your registered email address. We aim to process requests within 30 days.

Deletion may include:

  • Your Firebase authentication account and associated email address.
  • Your user profile and pending join requests.
  • Shelves you own, including their items, shopping lists, activity logs and invite codes, subject to the App's ownership and sharing rules.

Data held in backups or provider caches may remain temporarily before being deleted under applicable retention procedures. Information that must be retained by law may be handled as required by law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to delete it where required.
